### Footer Content

The dialog footer does not scroll along. It is therefore meant for actions and for notes that have to stay permanently visible, such as a footnote.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/TBG73nKNPgXcLTluGz6dLM/Doku--Dialog?node-id=4004-66&embed-host=share"
		description="Only place actions and permanently visible notes in the dialog footer."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="dont"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/TBG73nKNPgXcLTluGz6dLM/Doku--Dialog?node-id=4004-68&embed-host=share"
		description="Don't put further content in the dialog footer, it belongs in the content area. The footer does not scroll along and shrinks the scrollable area."
	/>
</div>

### Cancel Action

For a confirmation or an input, a cancelling action belongs in the dialog footer. The close button in the dialog header alone is not enough, because it is visually weaker and people expect both options as equal buttons in a decision.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/TBG73nKNPgXcLTluGz6dLM/Doku--Dialog?node-id=4024-2&embed-host=share"
		description="Place a cancelling action next to the confirming one in the dialog footer for a confirmation or input."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="caution"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/TBG73nKNPgXcLTluGz6dLM/Doku--Dialog?node-id=4024-4&embed-host=share"
		description="Don't offer cancelling only through the close button. Someone looking for the cancelling option finds only the confirming action in the dialog footer."
	/>
</div>

### Button Labels

In a confirmation, each button names its outcome. That way it is clear before the click what each of the two options does.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/TBG73nKNPgXcLTluGz6dLM/Doku--Dialog?node-id=4004-71&embed-host=share"
		description="Name the buttons of a confirmation after their outcome, such as “Delete file” and “Keep file”."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="caution"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/TBG73nKNPgXcLTluGz6dLM/Doku--Dialog?node-id=4004-73&embed-host=share"
		description="“Yes” and “No” leave the outcome open. Name the outcome instead."
	/>
</div>

### Action Order

The confirming action sits on the right, cancelling and additional actions to its left. That puts the action which completes the dialog at the end of the reading direction.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/TBG73nKNPgXcLTluGz6dLM/Doku--Dialog?node-id=4004-76&embed-host=share"
		description="Place the confirming action on the right and the cancelling one to its left."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="caution"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/TBG73nKNPgXcLTluGz6dLM/Doku--Dialog?node-id=4004-78&embed-host=share"
		description="A different order contradicts the reading expectation and makes people hit the wrong action. Keep the familiar arrangement."
	/>
</div>
