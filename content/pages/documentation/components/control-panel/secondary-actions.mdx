## Guidelines

<CollapsibleWrapper collapsedHeight="320px">

### Action Type

Secondary Actions hold supporting utility actions that are relevant across all pages but are not the user's primary task. Keep them visually subordinate to the navigation and Primary Actions.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/pElrqVUyojrzYzSagyJPS6/Doku--ControlPanel?node-id=4354-2&embed-host=share"
		description="Place utility actions (e.g. user profile, settings, help) that users may need occasionally."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="dont"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/pElrqVUyojrzYzSagyJPS6/Doku--ControlPanel?node-id=4354-4&embed-host=share"
		description="Do not place frequently used actions (e.g. search, notifications) in Secondary Actions. These belong in Primary Actions where they are immediately visible."
	/>
</div>

### Count

Keep Secondary Actions minimal. Too many icons at the end of the Control Panel create visual noise and compete with the navigation.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/pElrqVUyojrzYzSagyJPS6/Doku--ControlPanel?node-id=4354-6&embed-host=share"
		description="Use 1–3 utility actions that support the user without cluttering the navigation bar."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="dont"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/pElrqVUyojrzYzSagyJPS6/Doku--ControlPanel?node-id=4354-8&embed-host=share"
		description="Do not overload Secondary Actions with too many icons. A crowded end area makes the Control Panel feel heavy and unfocused."
	/>
</div>

### Flat Icon Limitation

The Flat Icon Control Panel does not support Secondary Actions. Utility actions like profile or settings should be placed as Flat Icon Navigation Items or moved into a dedicated page.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/pElrqVUyojrzYzSagyJPS6/Doku--ControlPanel?node-id=4354-10&embed-host=share"
		description="Use Top or Bottom Control Panel when secondary utility actions need a dedicated slot separated from navigation."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="dont"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/pElrqVUyojrzYzSagyJPS6/Doku--ControlPanel?node-id=4354-12&embed-host=share"
		description="Do not mix utility actions with navigation destinations in Flat Icon. Account or Settings can be a Navigation Item, but not an action button."
	/>
</div>

</CollapsibleWrapper>
