## Guidelines

<CollapsibleWrapper collapsedHeight="320px">

### Order on Desktop

On desktop, Primary and Secondary Actions sit next to each other, separated by a divider that appears together with the Secondary Actions. Without Primary Actions it separates an empty area.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/pElrqVUyojrzYzSagyJPS6/Doku--ControlPanel?node-id=4815-1398&embed-host=share"
		description="On desktop, fill the Primary Actions first and only use Secondary Actions when there is a hierarchy."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="dont"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/pElrqVUyojrzYzSagyJPS6/Doku--ControlPanel?node-id=4815-1402&embed-host=share"
		description="Don't use Secondary Actions without Primary Actions on desktop. Otherwise the divider separates an empty area."
	/>
</div>

### Action Type

When both areas are used, Secondary Actions hold the utility actions that are relevant across pages but are not the users' main task, such as user profile, settings, or help.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/pElrqVUyojrzYzSagyJPS6/Doku--ControlPanel?node-id=4354-2&embed-host=share"
		description="Place utility actions such as user profile, settings, or help in Secondary Actions when the Primary Actions are filled too."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="dont"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/pElrqVUyojrzYzSagyJPS6/Doku--ControlPanel?node-id=4354-4&embed-host=share"
		description="Don't place frequently used actions such as search in Secondary Actions. They belong in the Primary Actions."
	/>
</div>

### Count

Secondary Actions sit at the end of the Control Panel. The more actions sit there, the more crowded the area appears.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/pElrqVUyojrzYzSagyJPS6/Doku--ControlPanel?node-id=4354-6&embed-host=share"
		description="Place at most three actions in Secondary Actions."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="caution"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/pElrqVUyojrzYzSagyJPS6/Doku--ControlPanel?node-id=4354-8&embed-host=share"
		description="More than three actions overload the Control Panel. Check which actions are better placed on a dedicated page."
	/>
</div>

### Flat Icon Limitation

The Flat Icon variant has no area for Secondary Actions.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/pElrqVUyojrzYzSagyJPS6/Doku--ControlPanel?node-id=4354-10&embed-host=share"
		description="Choose a variant with an area for Secondary Actions when you need utility actions."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="dont"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/pElrqVUyojrzYzSagyJPS6/Doku--ControlPanel?node-id=4354-12&embed-host=share"
		description="Don't use Flat Icon when Secondary Actions are needed. The variant has no area for them."
	/>
</div>

### In the Drawer on Mobile

On mobile, Secondary Actions sit in the Drawer and are only reachable after opening it. Primary Actions stay visible on the first level.
