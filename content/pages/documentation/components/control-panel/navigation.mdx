## Guidelines

On Desktop, choose the variant that fits your navigation structure. On Mobile the Navigation in the Drawer is always Drill Down:

- **Popover:** Flat to medium structures; reach deeper levels with a single click
- **Tree:** Complex hierarchies (3+ levels) where the full structure stays visible
- **Drill Down:** Many Navigation Items with shallow depth (1–2 levels), shown one level at a time
- **Flat Icon:** A few equally weighted top-level destinations (2–6), accessed via icons

### Popover Horizontal Overflow

The horizontal Popover navigation in Desktop Top has limited space. Too many Navigation Items require scroll chevrons, which reduces the discoverability of the hidden ones.

### Tree Item Overload

Tree navigation is designed for complex hierarchical structures. When the navigation has many Navigation Items but a flat or shallow structure, Drill Down is more effective because it shows one level at a time and avoids overwhelming the user with a long expanded list.

### Drill Down Depth Limit

Drill Down shows one level at a time with a back-button pattern. With 4 or more levels, users lose orientation because they cannot see the full hierarchy. Tree keeps all levels visible and is better suited for deeply nested structures.

	/>

### Flat Icon Capacity

Keep the number of Flat Icon Navigation Items manageable. On Mobile, the slot is technically limited (2–4 with text, 2–6 without text). On Desktop there is no hard limit, but too many Navigation Items reduce clarity.
