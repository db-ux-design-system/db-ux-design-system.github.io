### Collapsible Control Panel Left

On desktop, users can collapse the Control Panel Left in the Drill Down and Tree variants; it always starts expanded. In the collapsed state, only the Brand and the button to expand it remain visible. This functionality is not available in the Flat Icon variant because the Control Panel is already at its minimum width.
