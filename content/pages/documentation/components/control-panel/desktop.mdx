## Guidelines

<CollapsibleWrapper collapsedHeight="320px">

### Position (Top vs. Left)

Choose the Control Panel position based on your application's navigation complexity. Top works for apps with few top-level Navigation Items; Left suits applications with many Navigation Items or deep hierarchies.

## Examples

<CollapsibleWrapper collapsedHeight="320px">

### Navigation Variant (Left)

The Left position supports four navigation variants: Popover for flat-to-medium hierarchies, Tree for deep structures with 3+ levels, Drill Down for less complex structures with many Navigation Items, and Flat Icon for quick icon-based access to a small set of destinations.

### Expand/Collapse (Left)

The Left Control Panel can be collapsed via a slide button to save screen space. It is always initially expanded. In the collapsed state, the navigation content is hidden and only the Brand and slide button remain visible.
