## Guidelines

<CollapsibleWrapper collapsedHeight="320px">

### Slot Content

Both slots hold either actions or environmental information. The elements of a slot read as one group, so only one of the two content types sits within a single slot. The Control Panel Brand End slot also holds environmental information. On mobile, it is the only slot available in the menu bar besides Actions 1.

### Order and Split

Fill Actions 1 first. Use Actions 2 in addition when the actions are separated by content or hierarchy. On desktop, Actions 2 shows a divider.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/pElrqVUyojrzYzSagyJPS6/Doku--ControlPanel?node-id=4815-1398&embed-host=share"
		description="Fill Actions 1 first and use Actions 2 for a second group of actions."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="dont"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/pElrqVUyojrzYzSagyJPS6/Doku--ControlPanel?node-id=4815-1402&embed-host=share"
		description="Don't fill Actions 2 as the only slot. On desktop the divider then separates an empty area."
	/>
</div>

### Count

Across both slots together, at most one main action and at most four utility actions apply. Which slot they sit in depends on the chosen split.

### Count on Mobile

On mobile, the slot in the menu bar shares its width with the Control Panel Brand. With an application name or additional content there is room for one action, without an application name for two icon buttons.

### Split by Page Type

The action split can follow the character of the page and the number of actions. The following variants show possible assignments, not a binding rule.

<h4>Marketing Page with One CTA</h4>

Place the main CTA in Actions 1 and leave Actions 2 empty.

	<GuidelineExample

	/>
</div>

<h4>Marketing Page with Additional Actions</h4>

Place the main CTA in Actions 2 and the remaining actions in Actions 1. The divider sets the CTA apart.

On mobile, Actions 1 stays visible in the menu bar, while Actions 2 sits in the Drawer footer and is only reachable after opening it.
