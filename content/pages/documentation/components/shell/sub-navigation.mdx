## Guidelines

On Desktop, choose the variant that fits your navigation structure. On Mobile only one flat level is supported, so the variants do not apply there:

- **Popover:** Flat to medium structures; reach deeper levels with a single click
- **Tree:** Complex hierarchies (3+ levels) where the full structure stays visible
- **Drill Down:** Many Navigation Items with shallow depth (1–2 levels), shown one level at a time

### Horizontal Overflow

The horizontal Sub Navigation has limited space. Too many Navigation Items require scrolling which reduces discoverability – especially on mobile where no scroll indicators are shown.

### Mobile: Single Level

The mobile Sub Navigation supports only one flat navigation level. Nested groups are not rendered.
