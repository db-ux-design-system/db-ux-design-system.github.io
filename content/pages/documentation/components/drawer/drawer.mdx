## Guidelines

<CollapsibleWrapper collapsedHeight="320px">

### Usage

The drawer does not block further work. It is therefore meant for content that is helpful but not required.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/y9c3hlB0PMdDYj3xLUCRWH/Doku--Drawer-%E2%9C%85?node-id=1-1132&embed-host=share"
		description="Use the drawer for contextual content that provides additional details."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="dont"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/y9c3hlB0PMdDYj3xLUCRWH/Doku--Drawer-%E2%9C%85?node-id=1-1133&embed-host=share"
		description="Don't use the drawer for critical or blocking information. Use a dialog or a dedicated page instead, because a task in the drawer cannot be enforced."
	/>
</div>

### Content

The drawer offers limited space. Multi-step flows and extensive validation are hard to complete and review inside it.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/y9c3hlB0PMdDYj3xLUCRWH/Doku--Drawer-%E2%9C%85?node-id=3-1110&embed-host=share"
		description="Place short, optional forms and clear actions in the drawer."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="dont"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/y9c3hlB0PMdDYj3xLUCRWH/Doku--Drawer-%E2%9C%85?node-id=3-1112&embed-host=share"
		description="Don't run multi-step processes or complex validation inside the drawer. The limited space hides the overall flow."
	/>
</div>

### Placement

The drawer overlays the entire page, regardless of the edge it appears from. That gives it the attention its content needs.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/y9c3hlB0PMdDYj3xLUCRWH/Doku--Drawer-%E2%9C%85?node-id=3-1113&embed-host=share"
		description="Let the drawer overlay the entire page width and height, including the header."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="dont"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/y9c3hlB0PMdDYj3xLUCRWH/Doku--Drawer-%E2%9C%85?node-id=3-1114&embed-host=share"
		description="Don't limit the drawer to a specific page region. As a partial area it reads like an embedded section rather than its own layer."
	/>
</div>

### Direction

`direction` defines the edge the drawer appears from. On mobile, that choice decides whether the content sits within thumb reach.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		imageRatio="portrait"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/y9c3hlB0PMdDYj3xLUCRWH/Doku--Drawer-%E2%9C%85?node-id=5011-2&embed-host=share"
		description="Choose `up` on mobile for short, interactive content."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="caution"
		imageRatio="portrait"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/y9c3hlB0PMdDYj3xLUCRWH/Doku--Drawer-%E2%9C%85?node-id=5011-3&embed-host=share"
		description="Across the full height, a few controls spread over the whole screen and the upper ones fall outside thumb reach. For long, scrollable content this does not matter."
	/>
</div>
