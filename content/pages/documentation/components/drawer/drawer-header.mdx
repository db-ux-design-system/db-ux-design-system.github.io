## Guidelines

<CollapsibleWrapper collapsedHeight="320px">

### Title

The title names the drawer. It can be set through `text` or placed as content in children.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/y9c3hlB0PMdDYj3xLUCRWH/Doku--Drawer-%E2%9C%85?node-id=4947-2&embed-host=share"
		description="Provide a title, either through `text` or as content in children."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="caution"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/y9c3hlB0PMdDYj3xLUCRWH/Doku--Drawer-%E2%9C%85?node-id=4947-3&embed-host=share"
		description="Without a title the drawer stays unnamed and assistive technologies only announce “dialog”. Only leave the title out when the content names itself."
	/>
</div>

### Title Wording

The title follows the label of the element that opened the drawer. It does not have to match word for word as long as it names the same thing.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/y9c3hlB0PMdDYj3xLUCRWH/Doku--Drawer-%E2%9C%85?node-id=4947-5&embed-host=share"
		description="Align the title with the label of the triggering element."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="caution"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/y9c3hlB0PMdDYj3xLUCRWH/Doku--Drawer-%E2%9C%85?node-id=4947-6&embed-host=share"
		description="If the title names something else, it stays unclear whether the expected drawer opened. The title and the label may differ in wording as long as they mean the same thing."
	/>
</div>

### Supporting Description

A short description can sit next to the title. It is only needed when the purpose of the drawer does not come across from the title alone.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/y9c3hlB0PMdDYj3xLUCRWH/Doku--Drawer-%E2%9C%85?node-id=4947-8&embed-host=share"
		description="Add a description when the purpose of the drawer does not come across from the title."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="caution"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/y9c3hlB0PMdDYj3xLUCRWH/Doku--Drawer-%E2%9C%85?node-id=4947-9&embed-host=share"
		description="A description that repeats the title only lengthens the way to the content. Leave it out in that case."
	/>
</div>

### Hierarchy

The title is set in bold by default. Additional content sits below it in regular weight and can optionally be de-emphasized further through size and color.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/y9c3hlB0PMdDYj3xLUCRWH/Doku--Drawer-%E2%9C%85?node-id=4947-11&embed-host=share"
		description="Set only the title in bold, additional content in regular weight and no larger."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="caution"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/y9c3hlB0PMdDYj3xLUCRWH/Doku--Drawer-%E2%9C%85?node-id=4947-12&embed-host=share"
		description="If additional content is bold or larger than the title, it competes with the title and weakens it as the entry point into the drawer."
	/>
</div>

### Start and End Slot

The start and end slot hold additional elements next to the title, such as a badge, an icon or an additional action. Both are hidden by default.
