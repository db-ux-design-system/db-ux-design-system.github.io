### Content

The drawer footer does not scroll along. It is therefore meant for the actions and for notes that belong to those actions.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample

### Arrangement

The drawer footer stacks its content vertically by default. For a horizontal arrangement the content can be grouped. Both arrangements are valid.
