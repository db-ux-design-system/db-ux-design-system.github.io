## Guidelines

### Spacing

The spacing between text blocks comes from one of two mechanisms. The text group derives it from the line height, while a container or stack sets it as a fixed gap through a `spacing` token.

	<GuidelineExample

		description="Use the text group when the spacing between headings and paragraphs should derive from the line height."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="dont"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/osMRGfMob0Zg1Oib7E7bob/Doku--Text-Group?node-id=1-1133&embed-host=share"
		description="Don't override the automatic spacing inside a text group with a manual gap. A fixed gap applies to every element alike and does not scale with their line height. Use a container or stack with a spacing token instead."
	/>
</div>

### Hierarchy

Headings and paragraphs together form the reading hierarchy of the text section. Each heading introduces its section, and the levels descend from top to bottom.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/osMRGfMob0Zg1Oib7E7bob/Doku--Text-Group?node-id=4003-12&embed-host=share"
		description="Structure the section with a clear order of heading levels followed by their paragraphs."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="dont"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/osMRGfMob0Zg1Oib7E7bob/Doku--Text-Group?node-id=4003-13&embed-host=share"
		description="Don't mix heading levels without a logical order. An unclear hierarchy makes the section hard to scan."
	/>
</div>

### One H1 per Page

The `h1` names the page as a whole and therefore appears only once, even with several text groups.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"

		description="Use a single h1 as the page title and h2 to h6 for the sections within the text groups."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="dont"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/osMRGfMob0Zg1Oib7E7bob/Doku--Text-Group?node-id=4003-16&embed-host=share"
		description="Don't place multiple h1 elements across the text groups of a page. Several top-level headings break the document structure and impair screen reader navigation."
	/>
</div>
