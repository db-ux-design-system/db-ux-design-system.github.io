- Group links by topic: each group gets a heading

## Guidelines

<CollapsibleWrapper collapsedHeight="320px">

### Secondary Content Only

Children holds only curated, secondary and further content. The primary navigation sits in the Control Panel.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/AtXL3DIkXMbr1PBEWB3yJG/Doku--Footer?node-id=4020-13456&embed-host=share"
		description="Place curated, secondary and further content in Children."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="dont"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/AtXL3DIkXMbr1PBEWB3yJG/Doku--Footer?node-id=4020-13632&embed-host=share"
		description="Don't reproduce the primary navigation in Children. Users then find the same destinations in two places and the hierarchy between primary and secondary navigation blurs."
	/>
</div>

### Number of Links

Up to seven links sit side by side in a single line. From eight links onward they are grouped by content.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/AtXL3DIkXMbr1PBEWB3yJG/Doku--Footer?node-id=4031-4104&embed-host=share"
		description="Arrange up to seven links in a single line and group them by content from eight links onward."
		fullWidth={true}
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="caution"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/AtXL3DIkXMbr1PBEWB3yJG/Doku--Footer?node-id=4031-4926&embed-host=share"
		description="More than seven links in one line become hard to scan. Group them by content instead."
		fullWidth={true}
	/>
</div>

### Group Headings

Each group of grouped links carries a unique heading that names its content.
