- Provide secondary navigation across all pages
- Close the layout: fill Footer Content and Footer Meta separately

## Guidelines

<CollapsibleWrapper collapsedHeight="320px">

### One Instance per Page

Exactly one Footer exists per page. A second instance creates a duplicate page ending and splits legal information across two places.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/AtXL3DIkXMbr1PBEWB3yJG/Doku--Footer?node-id=1-1132&embed-host=share"
		description="Use the Footer once per page."
		fullWidth={true}
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="dont"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/AtXL3DIkXMbr1PBEWB3yJG/Doku--Footer?node-id=1-1133&embed-host=share"
		description="Don't add a second Footer instance on the same page. The page ending appears twice and legal information is split across two places."
		fullWidth={true}
	/>
</div>

### Width in the Content Area

The Footer always spans the full width of the content area it sits in.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/AtXL3DIkXMbr1PBEWB3yJG/Doku--Footer?node-id=4004-1640&embed-host=share"
		description="Let the Footer span the full width of the content area."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="dont"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/AtXL3DIkXMbr1PBEWB3yJG/Doku--Footer?node-id=4004-5943&embed-host=share"
		description="Don't nest the Footer inside a narrower container. It then detaches from the edge of the content area and no longer reads as the page ending."
	/>
</div>

### Footer Meta When Legally Required

Footer Meta carries the application's legal information. If the application is required to display it, Footer Meta belongs in the Footer. Footer Meta defines which content that is.

<div className="guideline-examples">
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="do"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/AtXL3DIkXMbr1PBEWB3yJG/Doku--Footer?node-id=4004-6322&embed-host=share"
		description="Include Footer Meta when the application is required to display legal information."
	/>
	<GuidelineExample
		variant="dont"
		figmaUrl="https://embed.figma.com/design/AtXL3DIkXMbr1PBEWB3yJG/Doku--Footer?node-id=4004-10752&embed-host=share"
		description="Don't omit Footer Meta when the application is required to display legal information. The application then fails to meet its legal requirements."
	/>
</div>

</CollapsibleWrapper>
