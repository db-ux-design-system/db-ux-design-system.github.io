## Content matures from Concept to Stable

New content starts as **Concept**, becomes **Beta** and ends as **Stable**. **Legacy** and **Deprecated** mark content that is being replaced.

<LearnAside>

| Status     | Phase                        | In short                                 |
| ---------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Concept    | Active development lifecycle | Design reference, nothing is final       |
| Beta       | Active development lifecycle | Functional, minor changes still possible |
| Stable     | Active development lifecycle | Mature and mandatory as the UX standard  |
| Legacy     | Superseded or being removed  | Outdated, only critical bug fixes        |
| Deprecated | Superseded or being removed  | Removed in a future Major release        |

</LearnAside>

</LearnBlock>

<LearnBlock>

## Concept is a design reference

Concept content shows early directions, and **nothing is final**. Use it as a reference for your design, and plan for changes.

<LearnAside>

Designs and implementations may change substantially as ideas are tested and refined. The design is always available. A code implementation may not exist yet and usually follows in the Beta phase.

### What to expect

- **Design available**, the code implementation may follow later
- **No stability guarantees** – API, design and accessibility are not validated yet and may change at any time
- **Limited documentation** and usage guidelines
- **Plan for rework** as the content matures toward Beta and Stable

</LearnAside>

</LearnBlock>

<LearnBlock>

## Beta is ready to test in your project

Beta content is **functional and largely stable**, and design and development are aligned. Test it thoroughly and share your feedback.

<LearnAside>

Minor visual or functional changes may still occur. The content has gone through initial user testing and addresses a validated user need.

### What to expect

- **Aligned between design and development**
- Suitable for project use, but **minor Breaking Changes are still possible**
- Documentation and usage guidelines are available but may be incomplete
- Accessibility and cross-browser testing is **underway but not fully validated**

</LearnAside>

</LearnBlock>

<LearnBlock>

## Stable is the mandatory standard

Stable content is mature, accessible and brand-compliant. It is **mandatory to use** as the UX standard.

<LearnAside>

Stable content is updated and improved continuously, so it keeps meeting user needs and current standards.

### What to expect

- **Fully validated** for accessibility, cross-browser and cross-framework compatibility
- **Complete documentation**, usage guidelines and design specs
- **API stability** – Breaking Changes only happen in Major releases
- **Ongoing maintenance** and regular improvements

</LearnAside>

</LearnBlock>

<LearnBlock>

## Legacy and Deprecated content is being replaced

**Legacy** content only gets critical bug fixes. **Deprecated** content is removed in a future Major release – plan your migration.

<LearnAside>

### Legacy

Legacy content is outdated and no longer actively developed. Migrate to current content of the design system.

- **No new features** or improvements
- Only **critical bug fixes**
- A **recommended alternative** is available in the design system

### Deprecated

Deprecated content is marked for removal. In design, it is marked visually first, which gives you time to migrate before it is removed.

- **The content will be removed** – plan your migration
- **No bug fixes** or maintenance
- A **sufficient transition period** before the removal in a Major release

</LearnAside>

</LearnBlock>
