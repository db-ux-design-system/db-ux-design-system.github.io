## Why a Theme Builder?

The Theme Builder enables the easy creation and customization of themes based on the Design System. It is particularly valuable for product teams developing white-label products, allowing them to create their own color themes in just a few clicks while maintaining core design and accessibility standards.

<LearnAside>

In addition, teams can quickly generate the additional colors needed for DB products in a system-compliant way.

</LearnAside>

</LearnBlock>

<LearnBlock>

## Flexible color customization

Origin colors can be freely defined and adjusted at any time. Based on these colors, the theme is automatically recalculated and updated.

</LearnBlock>

<LearnBlock>

## Automatic color generation

The Design System automatically generates the required color variations from the defined origin colors, eliminating the need to manually maintain complex color systems.

</LearnBlock>

<LearnBlock>

## Accessibility built in

Accessibility requirements are considered during theme generation to help ensure sufficient contrast levels and a usable color palette across a wide range of use cases.

</LearnBlock>

<LearnBlock>

## Open source and freely available

The Theme Builder is available to all teams and can be used to create, customize, and evolve themes within the Design System.

**Note:** The DB Theme is not open source, but all newly created white-label themes are.

</LearnBlock>

<LearnBlock>

## When should the Theme Builder be used?

The Theme Builder is particularly useful when a product requires its own theme for business or domain-specific reasons. **Important:** The DB Theme is the standard for all DB products.

<LearnAside>

The Theme Builder is particularly useful when:

- a product requires its own theme for business or domain-specific reasons,
- white-label products intentionally need to differentiate from the DB brand,
- the existing DB Theme does not fully meet product requirements,
- additional or customized color themes are needed,
- accessibility requirements should be considered from the start,
- design and development teams want to work from a shared, system-compliant foundation.

Custom themes should only be introduced when there is a clear and justified need. The further a product diverges from the DB Theme, the more difficult it becomes to maintain a consistent, brand-aligned user experience.

</LearnAside>
